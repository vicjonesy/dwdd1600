Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital Citizenship is the ability to use technology and the internet responsibly, safely, and ethically.

What is Digital Law?

Digital Law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

An Acceptable Use Policy is a set of rules that explain how technology and internet resources are allowed to be used, especially in places like schools, workplaces, or organizations.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is when you use someone else's work, ideas, or words and pretend they are your own without giving proper credit.

It doesn't matter whether it's done on purpose or an accident-it's still plagiarism.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic Dishonesty is any action that gives a student an unfair academic advantage by breaking school or academic rules.

It involves being dishonest in assignments, tests, or academic work.

What are the rules for AI generated Content?

AI-generated content must be used according to school or organization rules. Students should not submit AI work as their own, must disclose AI use when required, and use AI only as a support tool (such as for ideas or editing). Misuse of AI is considered academic dishonesty.
